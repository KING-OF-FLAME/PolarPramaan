 Access 
 Access reference
 Every way in and out of OpenAlex — how to ask questions, how to get the data, what it costs, and how to fix what’s wrong.
 This reference is split into four sections:
 Querying. The ways to ask OpenAlex a question — all transpile to the same query object.
 Products. Every way to get the data — the website, a query, the CLI, an agent, the whole database, or Unpaywall.
 Pricing. How paying for OpenAlex works — the free tier, pay-as-you-go, annual plans and their benefits, and the PDF sync add-on.
 Fixing errors. How to report errors, fix them yourself, and get help.
